# Privacy Policy - Stall Booking

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Stall Booking is a private tool used by the organizers of the "Be Pakistani, Buy Pakistani" expo to record stall bookings. This policy explains what the app does with data.

## Data the app handles
- Booking details entered by the organizers: person name, brand name, brand items, events booked, payment amount and method, booking date and a contact phone number.
- Files uploaded by the organizers: the brand logo and the payment receipt photo.

## How Google data is used
- The app asks for permission to use Google Drive only for **files that the app itself creates** (the `drive.file` permission). It uses this to upload logos and receipts to a folder in the organizer's own Google Drive.
- Booking details are written to a Google Sheet owned by the organizer.
- The app cannot see or open any other file in the organizer's Google Drive.

## Sharing and storage
- Data is stored only in the organizer's own Google Sheet and Google Drive.
- Data is **not sold, shared with advertisers or given to third parties**.
- The app's access is protected by a password known only to the organizing team.

## Retention and deletion
Records are kept for as long as the organizers need them for the expo. To have a booking removed, contact the organizers, who can delete the row and the files from the Sheet and Drive. The organizer can also remove the app's access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
